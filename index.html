Services

View all services

Hajj Package

A service provided to Emirati customers wishing to perform Haj by registering on the smart application or website of the Authority to obtain approval for issuing a Haj permit after sorting the registrants and approving the names from the competent committee.

Start service

Registering Students to Learn the Holy Quran

A community service provided by the Authority for the teaching of the Holy Quran and its sciences, implemented through three semesters throughout the year, through Quran memorization centers, Masjid meetings, and the smart teaching platform.

Start service

Zakat Calculation

Thanks to this service, we are always ready to calculate your Zakat, determine its value and inform you of the Nisab value — the minimum amount of wealth above which Zakat becomes obligatory.

Start service

Endowment donation

This service allows individuals to donate electronically through digital channels, where the donor registers their data, chooses the type of project they wish to donate to, and specifies the amount.

Start service

Zakat Payment and Major Benefactors

Our staff are always ready to receive your Zakat, Zakat al-Fitr, and Sadaqah donations, whether in cash, cheques, credit or debit cards, Apple or Samsung Pay, through digital and traditional channels.

Start service

Disbursement of Zakat to Eligible Recipients

If you meet the requirements for Zakat entitlement, our employees are always ready to accept requests for interrupted or permanent financial assistance in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Start service

Mosques / Quran Centers Maintenance and Cleaning Notice

This service enables the public to participate in suggesting and commenting on mosque maintenance and cleanliness all over the UAE.

Start service

Submit an Inquiry

This service allows citizens, residents, visitors, and companies in the United Arab Emirates to submit their inquiries or request specific information from the Authority's organizational units.

Start service

Organizing Religious and Awareness Lectures for the Community

The Department of Preaching disseminates Islamic culture amongst the community by organizing lectures and seminars in schools, universities, and government and private entities.

Start service

Initiatives and activities

View all initiatives

Endowment for the Summer Program – Our Mosques: Safe Havens Of Faith

A sustainable developmental endowment designed to bolster the civilizational mission of the mosque, supporting Quranic education and summer activities for children, youth and families.

View more

Continuous charity, honoring your beloved mother

An investment endowment initiative acting as a centralized fund for philanthropic contributions from children, with income allocated to community development, orphan support and mosque construction.

View more

Childrens Endowment A Tribute to Fathers

In alignment with the Year of the Family, this endowment (Waqf) leaves a lasting legacy — proceeds support sustainable charitable projects, mosque construction and maintenance, and water provision.

View more

Endowment for Blessed Children

An endowment that begins with your child's birth and grows through your generosity — encouraging parents and relatives to establish a charitable endowment that evolves as the child grows.

View more
